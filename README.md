# Homework 9: Personal Ethics Statement
***Authenticity***: **Being true to one's beliefs without compromising humility**
* **Why**. I believe conformity is becoming an issue today, especially with social media. It is important that I understand and prioritize what my core values are to abstain from mob mentality that may cause destructive behavior. Additionally, I must not be too authentic where I see my beliefs as correct over others. I understand that values are subjective and it is important to comprehend others values.
* **What**. I always consider how I personally feel about something based on my beleifs before there is a chance for others thoughts on the same thing to impact how I feel about it. I often mentally take myself out of a situation and collect my thoughts before acting. I listen to others people values and compromise when necessary since there are no wrong values and my values are not above anyone elses. 
* **How**. When I catch myself agreeing with people without thinking. I will stop and consider my values before continuing, correcting myself if necessary. Before advocating on a position, I will ask: "Is this my observation, or am I repeating a script?" If I cannot explain the why behind the belief in my own words, I will refrain from acting on it. When I hear another persons values I consider and note why the person thinks that way rather than judging them for having that belief.
